Brand guidelines

Chain-of-custody traceability for organic shea, from the savannah of northern Ghana to the client. The brand should look as trustworthy as the record it carries: exact, calm and accountable.

Version 1.0 · 8 November 2026 · Savannah Fruits Company Limited

01 · Logo

Sun, horizon, route

A Shea-gold sun rising over the savannah horizon. Below it, the custody route runs from the origin lot to the destination lot: the place, the product and the chain in one glyph. The lots are square because every lot in Savannah Trace is a box with an ID.

  1. Sun: Shea Gold, radius 9 on a 32-unit grid.
  2. Horizon: Kernel Ivory, 25 × 1.8.
  3. Route: from the origin lot (Ivory) to the destination lot (Gold), lots 4.8 × 4.8.
  4. Tile: Baobab, radius 1.5. Square, never rounded further.

Lockups

Primary · white or Kernel Ivory
Reversed · Baobab or Charcoal
One ink · stamps and single-colour print

Wordmark: IBM Plex Sans, Savannah SemiBold + Trace Regular, tracking −1%. Letters are outlined in the SVG files.

Size and space

Minimum: the mark at 16 px or 5 mm; the lockup at 120 px or 30 mm wide.
Clear space: half the mark's height around the lockup; one lot width around the mark alone.

Do

  • 2 px corner radius on controls; 4 px is the maximum.
  • Active navigation: SemiBold label and a stronger icon on a transparent background.
  • Separate with 1 px Stone hairlines.
  • Use colour for state and data only.

Don't

  • Pills, capsules, lozenges or oversized rounding.
  • A tinted background, coloured left stripe or card around the active nav item.
  • Gradients or decorative shadows.
  • Shea Gold text on light backgrounds.

05 · Voice

Exact, calm, accountable

Write

  • "Received 4,250 kg at Tamba Butter Centre."
  • "Trace geometry is illustrative."
  • "Issued by Savannah Fruits Company Limited through Savannah Trace."

Avoid

  • "Seamless end-to-end visibility!"
  • "GPS-verified", "blockchain-secured".
  • Collector names on anything a client sees.

Two words, both capitalised: Savannah Trace. Sentence case for buttons and headings. Numerals with units. No exclamation marks or emoji.
